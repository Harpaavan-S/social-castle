Hydroelectic Lighting in the Industrial Revolution

In this project, I have made a 3D model of a castle in a lake on a floating island. The model is a direct representation of the first application of hydroelectric lighting. The inventor of this technology, William Armstrong, first implemented this system in his country home, Cragside House, a large, castle-like, mansion. The model aims to recreate that through the lit windows in the castle. Its location in a lake demonstrates the use of hydroelectric power to fuel this system. Because the model represents the first introduction of hydroelectric lighting, its position on a floating island in the sky showcases how nearly impossible it was to access it when it was first invented, with only the ultra-rich being able to afford it. Overall, my model presents a creative way to implement the introduction of hydroelectric lighting during the Industrial Revolution and showcases all the surrounding factors.
